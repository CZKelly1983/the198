The Map Is Not The Territory

The organisation has mistaken its model of the world for the world itself. A metric, a methodology, a playbook — something that was once a useful approximation of reality — has been elevated to the status of truth. Leaders in this zone are not stupid. They are precise in the wrong direction.

They optimise the instrument while the plane descends. The tell is not that the data is wrong — it is that no one is looking out the window anymore. When results stop coming, they do not question the model. They push harder against the wall, convinced that the logic is sound and the execution is the problem.
