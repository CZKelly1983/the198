DZ6

The Timing Trap

Too Early To Survive

The vision was correct. The timing was fatal. Companies in this zone did not misread the market — they arrived before the market existed, before the infrastructure could support them, before the customer behaviour had been trained into shape.

This is the most unjust of the Death Zones because the verdict of history often vindicates the idea while erasing the company. General Magic built the smartphone in 1992. Someone else launched it in 2007. The tragedy of the Timing Trap is not the failure. It is the footnote.
