What this is

The 198 is a forensic database of corporate failure. It is not a strategy playbook. It is not a listicle of business mistakes. It is a structured, scored, and interrogable record of how companies die.

Every company is assigned a Primary Death Zone — the dominant failure architecture — scored across ten forensic dimensions, and given a Lead Indicator: the one observable signal visible 18 to 24 months before total collapse.

The framework operates on one principle. Forensics over strategy. We identify why the plane crashed. We do not advise on paint colours for the wings.
