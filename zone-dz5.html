DZ5

The Relevance Horizon

The Slow Vanishing

The world moved. The business did not. This is not a failure of information — Kodak invented the digital camera. Blockbuster's CEO saw Netflix coming. The failure is structural: the business had organised itself around a specific version of customer need, and when that need evolved, the organisation could not follow.

Its cost base, its culture, its identity, its incentives — all anchored to a utility that was quietly evaporating. The Relevance Horizon is the point beyond which a business cannot see its own obsolescence. It is rarely sudden. It is almost always survivable with sufficient will. What kills the company is not the horizon but the refusal to look.
