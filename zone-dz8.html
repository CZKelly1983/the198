DZ8

The Human Ceiling

The Kill Vector

The organisation's fate was determined not by the market, the product, or the macro environment — but by the specific limitations, pathologies, or failures of the people at the top. This zone covers the full spectrum of human failure in leadership: the autocrat who drove the company off a cliff with unchecked conviction, the founder who could not scale beyond their own cognitive capacity.

The Human Ceiling is the point beyond which the company cannot rise because the person or people at the top will not allow it — whether through action, inaction, ego, or fear. The company could only ever go as high as its leaders permitted. DZ8 appears as a named factor in 75.8% of all 198 cases. This is not a calibration problem. It is the thesis.
